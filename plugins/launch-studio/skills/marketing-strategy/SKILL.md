# Marketing strategy

The goal is a plan a solo founder can run next week, built on evidence rather than slogans.

Reply in the user's language.

## Inputs

- `studio/brief.md` and `studio/research/competitors.md`. If the research is missing, suggest running `market-research` first. If the founder wants to go on without it, continue and mark the claims that depend on it as assumptions.
- Real customer evidence. Ask for it once: interview notes, waitlist numbers, analytics, sales calls, support messages.

## 1. One starting segment

Score the candidate segments from 1 to 5 on each of:
- pain intensity (with evidence);
- ability to pay;
- reachability (can we find them cheaply?);
- urgency (deadlines, seasons).

Choose **one** segment and list the others as "later". A product that is for everyone has a pitch nobody remembers.

## 2. Positioning and pitch

- Positioning: "For <segment> who <pain>, <product> is the <category> that <key benefit>. Unlike <main alternative>, it <difference backed by evidence>."
- A one-sentence pitch: at most 20 words, no jargon. The test: could a stranger repeat it to a friend?

## 3. Messages

- **Problem**, in the customer's own words, quoted from the research or interviews.
- **Promise**: a realistic outcome, never a guaranteed one.
- **Proof**: real proof only, such as a demo, numbers you can show, the founder's story or sourced facts. If there is none yet, write "none yet" and plan how to earn it.
- **Objections table** with the columns objection, evidence that it exists, answer, and where it is answered (landing page FAQ, onboarding, sales call).

## 4. Channels

1. List the channels where the segment demonstrably spends time. The evidence comes from the competitors' channels in the research, from communities and from search demand.
2. Score each channel on cost, time to the first result, fit, and the founder's own skill and time.
3. Pick 2–3 channels. Early-stage options usually include founder-led direct outreach, community participation, content and SEO (slow), partnerships (institutions, newsletters, communities) and small paid tests.
4. For each chosen channel, write the first concrete action for this week.

## 5. Test demand before spending

Run a cheap real test first:
- a waitlist with the real price shown;
- a pre-sale;
- a hands-on pilot done by the founder;
- 20 direct conversations.

Write the success number **before** the test runs, for example "at least 10% of landing visitors join the waitlist" or "3 of 20 calls agree to pay". Simulated buyer panels can help you prepare, but only real people decide.

## 6. The 30-day calendar

Write a table with the columns date, channel, action, asset needed, owner (founder or agent) and metric. Keep it realistic for a solo founder: at most one hour a day unless the founder says otherwise.

## 7. The first 10 hooks, posts and ads

- Each hook answers one objection or pain from the research.
- Each says where it will run and respects that place's character limits.
- All are marked `DRAFT`.

Rules for the drafts:
- No invented statistics, testimonials or user counts.
- No "#1", "best" or "guaranteed" without proof.
- No fake urgency; use only real deadlines.
- Follow the ad platforms' policies and local consumer and advertising law.
- Flag regulated topics for the founder: finance, health, children and minors, and promises about education or jobs.

## 8. Budget

The founder sets the total, because money is their gate. Split it across the test channels, each with a stop rule, for example "stop a channel when the cost per sign-up is above X after spending Y".

## 9. Numbers to watch

- The funnel: visitors → sign-ups → activated → paying, with a target for each stage.
- Where each number is measured. Analytics must follow the privacy and consent law (GDPR, KVKK, and others).
- A weekly 20-minute review that ends in keep, kill or change for each channel.

## 10. Not now

List the tempting things you are deliberately postponing.

## Privacy

Collecting e-mail addresses or running analytics needs a privacy notice, and often consent, under local law. Flag this for the founder, and add no tracker without their decision.

## Output

- `studio/marketing/strategy.md`: sections 1–5 and 8–10.
- `studio/marketing/calendar.md`: section 6.
- `studio/marketing/copy-drafts.md`: section 7, every item marked `DRAFT`.

Before any money is spent, suggest running `independent-review` on the strategy.
